# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and disclosure of private information. Respect a request to stop unwanted contact.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Enforcement considers context and gives affected contributors a chance to respond privately.

Report conduct concerns privately to Intesar Jawad through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/intesarjawad/smellcule/security/advisories/new). Do not post private reports in public issues.
